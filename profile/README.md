## 🚀 About Clover

**Clover** is transforming how supply chain teams and suppliers collaborate. We connect your people, processes, and data into one unified platform, making supplier relationships transparent, efficient, and intelligent.

## ✨ Why Clover?

✅ **Centralized Collaboration**  
- Chat, share files, and manage supplier conversations in one place.

✅ **Supplier Experience Reimagined**  
- Seamless onboarding and frictionless information exchange.

✅ **AI-Powered Insights**  
- Summaries, trends, price change alerts, and data validations—all with natural language AI.

✅ **Automated Workflows**  
- Automate onboarding, data requests, and other repetitive tasks.

✅ **Smart Search**  
- Search anything—products, suppliers, docs, or conversations—with contextual understanding.

✅ **Robust Integrations**  
- Connect with ERPs, PLMs, PIMs, Slack, Teams, and email for seamless workflows.
